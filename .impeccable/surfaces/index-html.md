# Surface brief: landing + monitor app (index.html)

Scope: the whole single-page app, landing gate plus the monitor workspace. Visitor mode: Operate (the landing is the app's entry gate and stays in the same world).
Audience/job: a developer or reviewer at a desk turns on their webcam, loads an on-device model, and reads a live focus verdict at a glance while working. Constraints: COOP/COEP isolation (same-origin assets only), no server, Korean UI, no invented accuracy claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The monitor is a signal head, not a dashboard. Three lamps always present, one struck; the verdict reads from across the room. Refuses the soft white SaaS card grid with green dots and the neon-on-black AI dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Matte signal-housing black (#101214-ish, warm) with visored lamp lenses; real signal chromaticities for the three states (blue-green go, amber, red) rendered as LED dot-matrix pictograms; everything else lives on auxiliary sign plates: white 보조표지 plates with black type and a guide-sign blue plate for setup. Barlow Semi Condensed (highway gothic lineage) + Pretendard; Doto dot-matrix for LED numerals only.

STORY: The visitor sees a lit signal head and understands the product is a live verdict; they learn it runs locally and what it costs to download; they load the model, start the camera, and watch the lamps change.
